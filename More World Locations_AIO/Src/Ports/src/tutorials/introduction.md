Ports let you ship cargo and, when enabled, teleport between discovered ports. Talk to a <color=orange>Port Manager</color> to discover a port.

<color=orange><b>Tabs</b></color>
<size=5>●</size> <color=yellow>Teleport</color> - Travel to a discovered port. This tab opens first when teleporting is enabled.
<size=5>●</size> <color=yellow>Manifest</color> - Purchase shipping chests. This tab opens first when teleporting is disabled.
<size=5>●</size> <color=yellow>Ports</color> - Choose a destination for your cargo.
<size=5>●</size> <color=yellow>Shipments</color> - Track outgoing cargo sent from this port.
<size=5>●</size> <color=yellow>Deliveries</color> - View and collect incoming cargo at this port.

<color=orange><b>Send and Collect Cargo</b></color>
<size=5>●</size> Buy a shipping chest and fill it with items.
<size=5>●</size> Open <color=yellow>Ports</color> and choose a port.
<size=5>●</size> Click <color=yellow>[Send shipment!]</color> to pay the fee and send your cargo.
<size=5>●</size> Open <color=yellow>Shipments</color> to check outgoing cargo and its arrival time.
<size=5>●</size> At the destination port, open <color=yellow>Deliveries</color>. Select a delivered shipment and click <color=yellow>[Open Delivery]</color>.
<size=5>●</size> Collect your items from the chests that appear near the Port Manager.

Favorites appear first in the Teleport and Ports lists. Both favorites and other ports are sorted alphabetically, ignoring letter case. Select a port and click <color=yellow>[Favorite]</color> beside Open Map.
