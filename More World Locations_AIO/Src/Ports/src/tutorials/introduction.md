Ports let you ship cargo and, when enabled, teleport between discovered ports. Talk to a <color=orange>Port Manager</color> to discover a port.

<color=orange><b>Tabs</b></color>
<size=5>●</size> <color=yellow>Teleport</color> - Travel to a discovered port. This tab opens first when teleporting is enabled.
<size=5>●</size> <color=yellow>Buy Shipment</color> - Purchase shipping chests, called manifests. This tab opens first when teleporting is disabled.
<size=5>●</size> <color=yellow>Send Shipment</color> - Choose a destination for your cargo.
<size=5>●</size> <color=yellow>Cargo</color> - Track incoming and outgoing shipments together, and collect deliveries at the current port.

<color=orange><b>Send and Collect Cargo</b></color>
<size=5>●</size> Buy a shipping chest and fill it with items.
<size=5>●</size> Open <color=yellow>Send Shipment</color> and choose a port.
<size=5>●</size> Click <color=yellow>[Send shipment!]</color> to pay the fee and send your cargo.
<size=5>●</size> Open <color=yellow>Cargo</color> to check its contents, destination, arrival time, and status. Rows marked <color=yellow>To</color> are outgoing; rows marked <color=yellow>From</color> are incoming.
<size=5>●</size> At the destination port, open <color=yellow>Cargo</color>. Select a delivered shipment and click <color=yellow>[Open Delivery]</color>.
<size=5>●</size> Collect your items from the chests that appear near the Port Manager.

Favorites appear first in the Teleport and Send Shipment destination lists. Each group is sorted alphabetically, ignoring letter case. Select a port and click <color=yellow>[Favorite]</color> beside Open Map.
