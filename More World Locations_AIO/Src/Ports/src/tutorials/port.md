The <color=yellow>Ports</color> tab lists discovered ports where you can send cargo.

<size=5>●</size> Talk to a Port Manager to discover a port. It then appears in the destination lists at other ports.
<size=5>●</size> Favorites appear first. Both favorites and other ports are sorted alphabetically, ignoring letter case. Teleport uses the same order.
<size=5>●</size> Select a destination to see its distance, estimated shipment time, and cargo details.
<size=5>●</size> Click <color=yellow>[Favorite]</color> beside Open Map to keep that port at the top. Favorites are saved with your character. Click <color=yellow>[Unfavorite]</color> to remove one.
<size=5>●</size> Buy shipping chests in <color=yellow>Manifest</color> and fill them with items.
<size=5>●</size> Select a destination and click <color=yellow>[Send shipment!]</color>. The shipping fee is deducted from your inventory.
<size=5>●</size> Open <color=yellow>Shipments</color> to track outgoing cargo. At its destination, use <color=yellow>Deliveries</color> to collect it.
