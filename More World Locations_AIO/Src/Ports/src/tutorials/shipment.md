The <color=yellow>Shipments</color> tab shows outgoing cargo sent from the <color=orange>current port</color> that your character can access.

<size=5>●</size> Select a shipment to see its origin, destination, contents, arrival time, and status.
<size=5>●</size> This tab tracks cargo. To collect it, travel to its destination and use <color=yellow>Deliveries</color>.
<size=5>●</size> Use <color=yellow>Ports</color> to send new cargo.
