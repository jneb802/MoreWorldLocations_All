The <color=yellow>Manifest</color> tab sells shipping chests. Fill these chests with the items you want to send.

<size=5>●</size> Select a chest to see its capacity and purchase requirements.
<size=5>●</size> Click <color=yellow>[Purchase]</color>. The chest appears near the Port Manager.
<size=5>●</size> Higher-tier chests become available as you discover more advanced materials.
<size=5>●</size> Each chest type can be purchased once per shipment, while the port has space.
<size=5>●</size> Open the purchased chests and put your items inside.
<size=5>●</size> Return to the Port Manager and open <color=yellow>Ports</color> to choose a destination.
<size=5>●</size> Empty chests cannot be sent.
