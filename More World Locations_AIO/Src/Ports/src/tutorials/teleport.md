The Teleport tab opens the <color=orange>Port Map</color>: the world map with every port you have discovered pinned and labeled.

<color=orange><b>Mouse & Keyboard</b></color>
<size=5>●</size> Left click a port pin, or a favorite in the sidebar, then confirm to teleport there
<size=5>●</size> Middle click a port to add or remove it as a favorite
<size=5>●</size> Hover over a port to see its distance and teleport cost in the sidebar
<size=5>●</size> Press <color=yellow>Escape</color> to return to the port

<color=orange><b>Gamepad</b></color>
<size=5>●</size> Pan with the left stick until a port sits under the crosshair, then press <color=yellow>$KEY_ButtonA</color> and confirm to teleport
<size=5>●</size> <color=yellow>$KEY_ButtonY</color> adds or removes the port under the crosshair as a favorite
<size=5>●</size> Right stick up/down walks the favorites sidebar; <color=yellow>$KEY_TabLeft</color> / <color=yellow>$KEY_TabRight</color> cycle through every port
<size=5>●</size> <color=yellow>$KEY_ButtonB</color> returns to the port

Your own map pins work as usual while the Port Map is open; only clicks on a port are taken over.

On worlds without a map, the Teleport tab lists your discovered ports instead: pick one and confirm.

<color=yellow>Note:</color> The teleport fee scales with distance and is shown before you confirm. Teleporting is a config option and may be disabled by the server.
